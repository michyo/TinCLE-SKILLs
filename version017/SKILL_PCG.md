# SKILL_PCG.md - TinCLE Guideline Skill [Platform Compliance & Content Safety Guidelines]

* **Copyright & License:** Copyright (C) 2026 michyo (Michiyo Tagami). All rights reserved.

## Overview
This skill enforces strict platform safety, legal compliance, accessibility standards, and rating zoning whenever generating game logic, assets, shaders, audio, UI, or scenarios for the **TinCLE** game engine.

---

## Category 1: General Audience Mode (Unrated / All-Ages Release)
Applied by default when no explicit mature/R18 intent is specified. Ensures zero-rejection releases on platforms like itch.io, Steam, App Store, Google Play, and web browsers.

### 1. Visual Safety & Photosensitivity (Epilepsy Prevention)
- **Flicker/Strobe Rate Limit:** Rapid brightness/luminance changes (flashes) MUST NOT exceed **3 Hz (3 flashes per second)** (ITU BT.1702-3 / TV Tokyo standards).
  - For flashes occupying >25% of screen area with >10% luminance change: Max 5 Hz, max 20% luminance transition, duration <= 2 seconds.
- **High-Risk Color Inversion:** High-speed alternating flashes of pure red, or high-contrast pairings like red/blue or red/black, are STRICTLY PROHIBITED.
- **Scene Transitions:** High-contrast transitions (e.g., pure black to pure white) MUST include a fade duration of at least **0.1s (6 frames @ 60fps)**.
- **Geometric Patterns:** Avoid full-screen high-contrast repetitive geometric patterns (stripes, concentric circles) moving/strobing rapidly.
- **Alternative Expressive Techniques for High-Intensity Requests:** If a user explicitly requests "intense flashing" or "psychedelic strobing", NEVER violate the 3 Hz limit. Instead, maximize visual intensity safely by increasing particle density, amplifying color saturation, adding dynamic camera micro-shakes, or layering high-impact sound effects (SFX).
- **Defensive Code Generation:**
  - Clamp screen-shake amplitude and flash intensity variables in code to safe threshold maximums.
  - Automatically generate global toggle flags in Options menu: `Reduce Screen Flashing (ON/OFF)` and `Reduce Screen Shake (ON/OFF)` (Xbox Accessibility Guidelines compliant).

### 2. Audio Safety & Distortion Prevention (Web/WASM/PC)
- **Digital Peak Clamping & Clipping Prevention:** Implement hard limiters/clampers so audio output amplitude NEVER exceeds **0 dBFS (1.0 peak amplitude)** to prevent digital clipping, distortion, or system-dependent volume spikes.
- **Voice Limiters:** Implement max simultaneous polyphony limits per audio category to prevent constructive amplitude summation (sudden loud bursts).
- **High-Frequency Filtering:** Apply automatic low-pass filters to synthesized Web Audio / PCM square or sawtooth waves exceeding **10 kHz** to protect hearing.

### 3. Cognitive & Visual Comfort
- **Trypophobia & Excessive Realism:** Avoid dense, high-contrast cluster patterns (trypophobia triggers) or hyper-realistic/photorealistic insect or gore depictions generated by AI. Use stylized/symbolic representations.
- **Subliminal Content:** Inserting sub-perceptual single-frame images or text designed to trigger unconscious distress is STRICTLY PROHIBITED.

### 4. Violence & Gore Control (Target-Specific Rules)
- **Humans & Real Creatures:**
  - ALLOW: Brief hit-spark/impact blood splatters.
  - PROHIBITED: Full-screen blood splatters, permanent wall/floor blood decals, dismemberment/decapitation, visible internal organs, or torture.
  - Substitute graphic corpse sprites with character-chip state changes, text, or audio cues.
- **Zombies & Non-Human Monsters:**
  - ALLOW: Gameplay-functional dismemberment (limb-break systems).
  - Use non-human blood colors (green, black, purple) or instant ash/evaporation visual decay logic to reduce graphic realism.
- **Robots, Machines & Inanimates:**
  - FULLY ALLOWED: Sparks, black oil, flying mechanical parts, gears, and structural destruction (not categorized as gore).
- **Crimes Against Minors & Animals:**
  - STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Gratuitous violence, abuse, or fatal injury against human minors (children) or animals under ANY circumstances (immediate platform ban trigger).

### 5. Legal, Intellectual Property & License Compliance
- **Hate Speech & Bias:** Exclude discriminatory, offensive, or absolute political/religious assertions targeting protected characteristics.
- **Trademark & Parody Avoidance:**
  - NEVER generate exact or 1-letter-altered parodies of trademarked names (e.g., `Mintendo`, `PrayStation`).
  - Replace IP-bound terminology with generic equivalents (e.g., replace `PokeBall` with `Capture Capsule`; generic terms like `Potion` or `Magic` are permitted).
- **Visual IP Disambiguation:**
  - Do NOT generate characters combining signature color schemes and silhouettes of known IPs (e.g., red cap + blue overalls + mustache). Deconstruct and re-render as unique original designs.
  - Do NOT copy exact UI layouts or icon sets from commercial games. Build custom procedural layouts using TinCLE rendering calls.
- **Public Domain Utilization:** Actively leverage public domain works (mythology like Zeus/Odin, fairy tales, classical literature) for lore and motifs as zero-risk, universally recognizable assets.
- **TinCLE Attribution & Packaging Rigor:**
  - Ensure all third-party and generated assets/libraries used are accurately logged in `CREDITS.txt` as required by TinCLE licensing.
  - When packaging the game for release (e.g., creating release ZIP archives or platform builds), MANDATE that `CREDITS.txt` and `AI_DISCLOSURE.md` (for Steam/itch.io AI forms) are strictly included in the root directory without omission.

## Category 2: Explicit Intent Mode (Gore / Adult / Horror Opt-In)
Triggered ONLY when the user explicitly requests "Gore", "R-18 / Adult", or "Extreme Horror" in the prompt. Respects creator freedom while strictly maintaining non-negotiable legal/platform boundaries.

### 1. Unlocked Features (Upon Explicit User Request)
- Realistic 2D/3D blood impact effects and persistent decal decal systems (blood staying on floors/walls).
- Meaningful gameplay dismemberment, decapitation, and damage visual models for zombies, monsters, and adult human characters.
- Graphic depiction of hopeless, grim, or fatal narrative themes as core gameplay or horror elements.

### 2. Absolute Non-Negotiable Prohibitions (Zero Tolerance)
The following are STRICTLY PROHIBITED even under explicit R-18/Gore mode due to international law and platform instant-ban policies:
1. **Real-World Crime & Atrocity Reenactment:** Generating scenarios or gameplay celebrating or accurately recreating real-world terrorism, massacres, or sensationalized crimes.
2. **Violence or Sexual Depiction Involving Minors:** Any physical, sexual, or lethal violence toward children or child-like characters under any setting (triggers law enforcement reporting and permanent account bans).
3. **Interactive Detailed Torture:** Player-controlled, step-by-step execution of explicit torture mechanics (e.g., pulling fingernails, electrocution). Must be handled via fade-to-black, implied text, or non-interactive cutscene cues to prevent high-rate store rejection.

### 3. Mandatory Zoning & Player Protection
- **Content Warning (CW) Screen:** Automatically inject a skippable `Content Warning` initial frame upon game startup detailing high-intensity themes (e.g., Graphic Gore, Horror).
- **Safety Toggles in Options:** Automatically implement settings toggles: `Gore Effects (ON/OFF)` and `Blood Color (Red / Black / Green)` to allow player-side accessibility adjustment.
